Selected Projects

Leiomyosarcoma Recurrence Risk Modeling & App

Identified predictors of early recurrence using logistic, Cox, and Fine-Gray competing risks models; built a Shiny app enabling the oncology team to generate custom cumulative incidence curves; contributed unit tests and documentation to the REDCapSync R package.

Cannabis Use & Biological Aging

Survey-weighted GLM and random forest analyses on nationally representative NHANES data examining cannabis-aging associations across survey cycles; contributed to the nhanesdata R package for reproducible cross-cycle workflows.

Heavy Metal Exposures & Parkinsonism

Applied WQS mixture modeling, regularization, and multivariable methods to evaluate heavy metal exposure-parkinsonism associations; assisted design of a PostgreSQL database for an NIH-funded longitudinal study on WTC first responders.

Enhancing Nurse Anesthesia Education

Employed proportional odds and multinomial logistic regression comparing student perceptions across novel vs. traditional training modalities as well as attitudes toward artificial intelligence in nurse anesthesia education; contributed to survey design and statistical analysis for program evaluation and manuscripts.
